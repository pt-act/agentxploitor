## The Pitch

AgentxploiTor is the first **independent** autonomous AI security platform for smart contracts and Farcaster miniapps — powered by 19 specialist AI agents that learn and improve with every audit. We are building the trust infrastructure for the Base and Farcaster ecosystem.

---

## The Problem

Security auditing in Web3 has three structural failures:

| Failure | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Conflict of interest** | Audits paid for by the project being audited — reports written to satisfy, not protect |
| **Static** | Done once at launch, never updated as code evolves — exploits happen months later |
| **Inaccessible** | $20,000–$100,000 per engagement — developers can't afford it, end users can't access it |

The consequence: billions in capital flow into unverified, potentially vulnerable systems. When exploits happen, trust collapses — not just in the project, but in the ecosystem.

---

## The Solution

An on-demand, independent, AI-powered security auditing platform that:

1. **Proves vulnerabilities work** — visual proof, not theoretical reports
2. **Is structurally independent** — paid by the user seeking truth, not the project seeking endorsement
3. **Learns from every audit** — 19 AI agents with persistent memory and self-evolving skills
4. **Covers two critical surfaces** — smart contracts (EVM + Solana) and Farcaster miniapp frontends
5. **Lives where users are** — native Farcaster miniapp, Base chain payments

**Self-Improving Intelligence:**

**The compound effect:** every audit makes the platform smarter for every future user. The intelligence moat grows with every job.

## The Independence Moat

Every report carries an immutable independence declaration with the requesting Farcaster FID and timestamp. This is structurally impossible for traditional audit firms to replicate — they are paid by the project. We are paid by the user who wants the truth.

As audits accumulate, a **public registry of independently verified contracts** forms naturally — the trust layer the ecosystem needs.
