## The Problem

### Security Auditing is Broken

- **Manual & Slow**: Traditional audits take weeks, cost $50K+
- **Incomplete Coverage**: Human auditors miss vulnerabilities
- **No Proof**: Reports are theoretical — exploits may not actually work
- **Reactive**: Audits happen post-deployment, not during development

- **$4.2M**: Average cost of a data breach in 2023
