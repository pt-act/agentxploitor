# Farcaster Integration

AgentxploiTor is built as a native Farcaster miniapp. Every audit is authenticated with your Farcaster identity — giving you verifiable attribution on every report you generate.

## Authentication

AgentxploiTor uses **Farcaster Quick Auth** — the standard authentication method for Farcaster miniapps. No separate account needed. Your Farcaster identity is your account.

```
Open AgentxploiTor in Farcaster
        ↓
Quick Auth: sign with your Farcaster account (one tap)
        ↓
JWT issued — valid for your session
        ↓
Your FID (Farcaster ID) is recorded on every audit you request
```

## Your FID on Every Report

Every audit report generated through AgentxploiTor carries your Farcaster FID as immutable attribution:

```
Requested by: Farcaster FID [XXXX]
Timestamp:    2026-03-04T09:00:00Z
```

This serves two purposes:

1. **For you**: proof that you commissioned this independent audit — useful for responsible disclosure, community reporting, or portfolio documentation
2. **For the ecosystem**: a verifiable, public record of who requested the audit — building trust in the registry over time

## Responsible Disclosure via Farcaster

When you audit a public miniapp in Research mode and find significant vulnerabilities, AgentxploiTor generates a responsible disclosure template pre-formatted for Farcaster:

- Non-sensitive finding summary (severity, category, recommended fix)
- Your FID as reporter attribution
- Pre-filled Farcaster cast composer link — one click to notify the developer
- Optional: direct Farcaster DM link to the miniapp creator (if their FID is resolvable)

## Miniapp Target Discovery

When you input a plain miniapp name, AgentxploiTor searches the Warpcast API for frames and miniapps matching that name — surfacing candidates for you to confirm before the audit begins. This means you can find and audit any Farcaster miniapp directly from within AgentxploiTor, without needing to know its URL.

## Rate Limiting

To protect the integrity of the audit queue, rate limits apply per FID:
- Research mode (auditing others' apps): moderate limits
- Self-audit mode (auditing your own app): generous limits
- Contract audit mode: generous limits

Rate limits reset daily. They exist to prevent abuse, not to restrict legitimate use.
