# The Independence Model

The most important word in security auditing is not "autonomous" or "intelligent" — it is **independent**.

## The Problem With Traditional Audits

Every traditional security audit has a structural conflict of interest: the project being audited commissions and pays for the audit. The auditor's continued business depends on maintaining the relationship with that client.

This does not mean auditors are dishonest. It means the system creates pressure — subtle and sometimes not so subtle — toward conclusions that do not embarrass the client. Critical findings get softened. Scope gets narrowed. Reports get written to satisfy, not to protect.

**The person most affected — the end user risking their capital — has no seat at the table.**

## The AgentxploiTor Model

AgentxploiTor inverts this completely.

**Who pays for the audit:** The person who wants the answer — investor, developer, researcher.

**Who benefits from a finding:** The user who paid. Not the project.

**Who has a financial relationship with the target:** No one. AgentxploiTor has no affiliation with any audited project.

This is not a policy. It is structural. The business model makes conflict of interest impossible.
